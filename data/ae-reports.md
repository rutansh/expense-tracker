# FICTIONAL ADVERSE EVENT REPORTS (demo data - not real patients)

## Case 1
62-year-old female on ZX-101 50 mg daily for 5 weeks. Presented to the ED with
severe abdominal pain; diagnosed with appendicitis and hospitalized for an
appendectomy. Recovered after surgery. Reported by treating physician, who
considers the event possibly related to the study drug.

## Case 2
45-year-old male on ZX-101 for 3 weeks. Reported mild headache lasting two days,
resolved without treatment. Reported by the patient. Relationship not assessed.

## Case 3
58-year-old male on ZX-101 for 10 weeks. Found unresponsive at home and pronounced
dead on arrival at hospital. Cause of death unknown; no autopsy performed.
Reported by a family member. Relationship not assessed.

## Case 4
51-year-old female on ZX-101 for 8 weeks. Developed cellulitis of the left lower
leg requiring IV antibiotics and a 4-day hospital stay. Recovering at time of
report. Reporter (nurse) considers the event unlikely related to the study drug.
